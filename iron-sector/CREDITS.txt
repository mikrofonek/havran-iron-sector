Original recordings used:
1911/A_34P.wav — pistol
Mossberg/N_30P.wav — shotgun
AR-15/D_24P.wav — automatic weapon shot sample

Edited for the game: first shot extracted, converted to mono 44.1 kHz
16-bit PCM, filtered, gain reduced, tail faded. For the fictional Vulcan,
a real AR-15 single-shot recording is repeated at the game's firing rate;
it is not a recording of a real rotary cannon.

All models, environment geometry and shader materials are original
procedural work made for this game.

Additional CC0 recordings in campaign version:
Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone), by Fantozzi; submitted by qubodup.
https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
Fantozzi-StoneL1.flac and Fantozzi-StoneR1.flac converted to mono WAV.

Gun reload sounds, by SpringySpringo (airsoft mechanisms), CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
gunreload1.wav, assaultriflereload1_0.wav, shotguncock_0.wav.
Converted to mono 44.1 kHz, high-pass filtered and gain adjusted.

Two takes of each firearm are alternated in the campaign version.
Original second-take offsets: pistol 6.64 s, Mossberg 4.95 s, AR-15 3.90 s.
Generated visual assets are described in ART-NOTES.md.

Original music score
15 original instrumental cues, procedurally composed and synthesized
for this game. Electric-bass, plucked-string, FM electric-piano and drum
timbres; unique tempo, key and motif for each mission. No external music
recordings or existing TV themes were used. Encoded to stereo MP3.
Track titles, tempos and durations: music/TRACKS.json.

Lea portrait: original AI-generated image, OpenAI image generation. Weapon preview SVGs and six painting motifs: original project artwork.
