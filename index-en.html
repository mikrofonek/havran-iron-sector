How I built it with AI

I specified requirements, played the prototype and gave concrete feedback. AI implemented changes and prepared automated tests. The repository includes my original Czech prompts, decisions revised after playing, and verification records across versions.

Version 5.3 verification

210 automated acceptance cases, 140 regression scenarios per edition and 120 short browser performance cases. Measured gameplay scenes ran at about 60 FPS on Apple M1 in Edge at 1440 × 900. The reports document the conditions and limits.
